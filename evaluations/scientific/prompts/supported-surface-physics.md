Model a one-dimensional stack containing 10 nm of silicon dioxide and 1 µm of p-type silicon doped at `1e16 cm^-3`, at 300 K. Use a 4.10 eV metal gate on the oxide and an ohmic back contact. At the oxide-silicon interface use electron and hole surface capture velocities of `1e4 cm/s`, plus a single acceptor trap level with density `5e11 cm^-2` and energy `0.15 eV` above the intrinsic level. Sweep the gate from `-1 V` to `+1 V` in `0.25 V` steps. Show potential, carrier density, interface charge, trap occupancy, surface recombination rate, and terminal-current conservation.
