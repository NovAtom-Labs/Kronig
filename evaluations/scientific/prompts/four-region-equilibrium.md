Study a continuous 2 µm silicon structure at 300 K with four regions in order: 0.2 µm at 5 × 10¹⁶ cm⁻³ acceptor concentration, 0.8 µm at 1 × 10¹⁶ cm⁻³ acceptor concentration, 0.8 µm at 1 × 10¹⁶ cm⁻³ donor concentration, and 0.2 µm at 5 × 10¹⁶ cm⁻³ donor concentration. Use zero-bias ohmic contacts. Calculate equilibrium potential, electric field, electron density, hole density, and terminal current, then verify current balance and numerical convergence.
