At 300 K, study a one-dimensional 20 nm silicon-dioxide layer between two electrostatic contacts. Apply a 10 mV small-signal excitation at 1 kHz to the left contact while the right contact remains the reference. Report the real and imaginary terminal current density, complex admittance, conductance, and capacitance per unit area. Also preserve the electric potential and field used for the operating point, and validate the result against the parallel-plate limit.
