Investigate how a graded donor implant changes the electrostatic field and carrier transport in a one-dimensional silicon structure. Work in this folder and keep the simulation reproducible.

Use a 2 micrometre silicon region with uniform boron background doping of 2e16 cm^-3 and a Gaussian phosphorus implant with peak concentration 8e17 cm^-3 centred at 1.7 micrometres from the left boundary. Use ohmic contacts at both ends. Compare the electrical response at -0.5 V, 0 V, +0.2 V, and +0.4 V, with the other contact grounded.

Show the doping, potential, electric field, electron and hole concentrations, space charge, band profiles, carrier mobility, recombination, and terminal current density as interactive results. Use drift-diffusion, doping-dependent mobility and Shockley-Read-Hall recombination where appropriate. Check convergence, current conservation, and whether the field and charge profiles are physically consistent. Ask me for missing physical inputs before making the plan.
