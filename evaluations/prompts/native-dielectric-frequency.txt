Study the small-signal response of a 20 nm silicon dioxide film between two ideal electrodes at 300 K. Both electrodes are initially at zero volts. Drive the left electrode with a 10 mV small signal, with the right grounded, over a logarithmic frequency sweep from 1 kHz to 1 MHz using seven points. Plot capacitance per unit area, complex admittance, and the phase of the current response. Compare capacitance against the parallel-plate value and show interactive plots. Keep the setup reproducible in this folder.
