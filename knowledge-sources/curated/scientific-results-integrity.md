# Carrier statistics, units and numerical evidence

For any supported one-dimensional structure, choose carrier statistics using the intended operating conditions, not only bulk doping. Applied bias can cause accumulation or injection with local carrier populations comparable to the effective density of states, even in lightly doped material. Fermi statistics must be considered when degeneracy is plausible. An explicit researcher choice must not be silently replaced. The DEVSIM adapter's Fermi law is the identified matched Joyce-Dixon/Sommerfeld approximation described in `devsim-fermi-transport.md`, not exact integral quadrature or a guarantee of experimental accuracy.

The local Boltzmann applicability check uses solved n/Nc and p/Nv. The conservative engineering acceptance limit is 0.1 at every valid sample. This threshold is a product criterion for the low-population regime, not an experimentally calibrated universal cutoff. The leading order-half Fermi correction is approximately the population ratio divided by 2^(3/2). Missing required populations or density-of-states evidence cannot establish applicability. The nondegenerate derivation is explained by the TU Wien semiconductor transport reference: https://www.iue.tuwien.ac.at/phd/mwagner/node28.html .

Numerical convergence, physics applicability and mesh stability answer different questions. A converged nonlinear solve only shows that the discretized equations reached their specified tolerance. Integrated charge balance uses the native Poisson control volumes and contact displacement flux. Fixed-potential contact nodes replace bulk Poisson rows, so counting their source volume a second time is incorrect. Interface source-charge sign conventions must be retained and identified. This discrete consistency check does not independently prove spatial resolution.

Mesh verification halves region spacing, explicit anchors and automatically resolved profile landmarks while preserving physical inputs, contact conditions, study coordinates and requested outputs. Compare spatial fields inside each material region. Do not interpolate across dielectric-permittivity jumps or duplicate interface nodes. The acceptance limit is 3 percent per response/profile, with recorded dimensional absolute noise floors for near-zero quantities. Up to four refinements may be attempted within the total execution budget. Failed refinement, missing evidence or budget exhaustion must remain visible. One passing mesh pair is an error indicator, not proof of asymptotic convergence or experimental calibration. Preserve original and refined specifications, native evidence, compiled files, versions and hashes.

Keep canonical results in SI. Requested display units belong to `ExperimentSpec.display_units` and must be dimensionally compatible with the requested quantity. Presentation conversions change plot/table/export values, not underlying canonical data or validation inputs. F/m^2 to F/cm^2 multiplies by 10^-4; m^-3 to cm^-3 multiplies by 10^-6. Capacitance density cannot be converted to absolute capacitance without a researcher-provided contact area. Failed or missing samples must be marked unavailable rather than published as zero. Complex responses retain their real and imaginary components.

Implementation references: `domain/presentation.py`, `results/presentation.py`, `validation/statistics.py`, `validation/charge.py`, `validation/refinement.py`, DEVSIM compiler 0.2.5. These are reviewed engineering guidance and executable checks, not a replacement for material-model calibration or experimental measurements.
